Useful answers

Questions, answered.

Is this the live Minarva Biz customer application?

No. This is a visual design-review preview that illustrates the marketing layout and sample product workflows. The customer application is a separate Next.js service undergoing Cloudflare acceptance.

Can I use Minarva Biz without internet access?

The Windows application follows a local-first architecture. Features that call cloud services or synchronize need internet access. Customer acceptance of each edition is ongoing.

Is customer registration available?

Self-service registration, trial automation and some production integration checks are not yet complete. Existing authorized customers may use their login link.

Are the charts and sales figures genuine customer data?

No. All figures, sample invoice lines and workflows in this preview are illustrative. No customer data or testimonials are used.
