# Minarva Biz — Competitor Capability Baseline

**Purpose:** Living benchmark for product-gap research.  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-02  
**Rule:** Benchmark capabilities and workflows. Do not copy proprietary code, visual design, branding or protected content.

## 1. Vyapar baseline

Official Vyapar material reviewed in October 2026 shows a product/economy broader than basic invoicing.

### Core business capabilities observed
- GST-ready billing/invoicing;
- accounting;
- inventory tracking;
- quotations;
- sales/purchase orders;
- expenses;
- receivables/payables;
- cash-flow and tax reports;
- multiple companies/firms;
- multi-device sync;
- multiple item prices;
- party-specific rates;
- godown/warehouse management;
- stock transfer;
- fixed assets;
- bill-wise and party-wise profit/loss;
- low-stock alerts;
- item custom fields;
- TCS/TDS support;
- e-Invoice/e-Way Bill;
- barcode generation/import/scanning;
- OCR for purchase bills;
- POS/payments;
- weighing-scale integration;
- service reminders;
- account statements;
- Tally import/export;
- accountant access;
- customer credit limits;
- loyalty points;
- greetings/offers;
- transaction SMS;
- WhatsApp integration;
- bulk WhatsApp;
- marketing tools;
- online store;
- custom label printing;
- manufacturing transactions;
- BOM/recipe;
- material consumption;
- production cost;
- manufacturing/consumption reporting;
- batch/expiry use cases.

### Industry positioning observed
Vyapar publicly markets solutions for areas including:

- retail;
- pharmacy;
- grocery;
- restaurant;
- jewellery;
- clothing/apparel;
- manufacturing.

### Wider Vyapar ecosystem observed
Official guides list:

- Vyapar App;
- Vyapar TaxOne;
- Vyapar Flyy;
- Vyapar Table;
- NeoDove telecalling CRM;
- Vyapar Thermal Printer.

### Implication for Minarva
Vyapar parity is not reached by invoice + inventory alone. The Minarva registry must cover core business operations, industry workflows, communication/marketing, commerce, hardware and ecosystem services.

## 2. QuickBooks / Intuit benchmark

Current official QuickBooks/Intuit material shows an AI-first finance direction.

Capabilities to benchmark:

- conversational business Q&A;
- AI business insights;
- conversational report builder;
- custom automations;
- file analysis;
- accounting AI;
- transaction categorization;
- bank matching;
- anomaly/review assistance;
- workflow execution through natural language;
- payroll actions where supported;
- intelligent onboarding;
- proactive automation suggestions.

### Minarva response
Build finance AI around deterministic accounting validation and approval policies. Minarva must add local-first/offline and industry operations that QuickBooks Online does not target in the same way.

Capability areas visible in current product updates include:

- POS;
- payments;
- inventory;
- invoices;
- online;
- orders;
- appointments;
- customers;
- loyalty;
- marketing;
- payroll/staff;
- reports;
- banking/money;
- franchises;
- KDS;
- sales channels.

Square AI direction includes Managerbot: a proactive operational agent that monitors the business, surfaces issues and automates routine work. Square also integrates merchant discovery/order experiences into external AI assistants.

### Minarva response
Add proactive operational intelligence and maintain provider-neutral integration surfaces for future AI-commerce discovery.

## 7. Xero benchmark

Xero is a benchmark for global cloud accounting and accountant workflows.

Current AI direction includes JAX:

- bookkeeping assistance;
- routine-work automation;
- cash-flow support;
- financial intelligence;
- integrations that bring live financial insight into external productivity tools.

### Minarva response
Build comparable financial intelligence, while keeping business operations, POS, industry packs and offline capability inside the same platform.

## 8. Stripe / SaaS control-plane benchmark

For Minarva's own commercial model, modern subscription platforms demonstrate capabilities that the internal Control Plane should support independently of payment provider.

Required concepts:

- checkout/payment links;
- trials;
- subscriptions;
- plan changes;
- scheduled changes;
- prorations;
- discounts/coupons;
- invoices/credit notes;
- customer portal;
- entitlements;
- per-seat pricing;
- tiered pricing;
- usage-based pricing;
- prepaid credits;
- revenue recovery;
- payment retry;
- cancellation flows;
- migration between billing providers.

Minarva must model subscriptions and entitlements internally so payment providers can be replaced.

Community reports may be used to discover pain points, but product capability claims should be verified against authoritative sources where possible.
