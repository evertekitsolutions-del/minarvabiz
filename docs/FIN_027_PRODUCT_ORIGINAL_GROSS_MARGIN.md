# FIN-027 — Product-wise Original Gross Margin (vertical cut)

The Web and Windows Reports panel contains a manager-only **Product Margin** tab derived from existing paid or partially paid issued sales, with account permission and branch boundaries at the domain layer. Rows contain SKU, posted invoice count, aggregate sold quantity to 0.001, sales excluding tax, historical sale-line cost, original gross profit, and percentage. Date filters and existing CSV / Excel / PDF report export use the same approved rows.

The snapshot calculation uses original invoice line unit prices, discounts and sale-time costs; it refuses invoices with unallocated discounts, missing costs or inconsistent line/tax totals. Distinct measured cuts of the same product aggregate by product ID without losing invoice count.

**Service type cut:** A second restricted **Service Margin** tab reports delivered tailoring, alteration, wholesale, uniform, wedding dress and T-shirt orders by service type. It uses the order's already discounted net price, recorded material cost and order-specific expenses. It excludes cancelled, deleted or non-delivered orders. Grouped service reports have the same date filtering and CSV/Excel/PDF export. Printing cost already included in the stored order-specific expenses must not be counted a second time.

**Registry status: partial FIN-027.** Both product and delivered-service original/direct-cost margin views exist, but neither is final statutory net P&L. They exclude later returns, refunds, incentives, taxes where not separated, operating overhead, labor/payroll allocation and other unallocated adjustments.
